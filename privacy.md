# Privacy policy: Claude Work Access

Claude Work Access is a private tool used only by its owner, aijkwork@gmail.com. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses: files in its owner's Google Drive, to read and save the owner's own work files on the owner's instruction.

What it collects: nothing. It does not collect, sell or share personal data, and it has no users other than its owner.

Where data goes: files stay in the owner's Google Drive. The access key is kept privately by the owner.

Revoking access: the owner can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: aijkwork@gmail.com
